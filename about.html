The Science Behind mag2

Two years in development. Zero plastic. Engineering the world’s first monolithic magnesium speedcube.

Origin Story

Why mag²?

The Name

Magnesium × Magnets

The name mag² (mag-squared) is a direct play on our two core technological pillars: Magnesium, the ultra-lightweight aerospace alloy we laser 3D-print, and Magnets, the 48 embedded neodymium positioning cores driving every turn.

Timeline

Founded 2024 • 2 Years in R&D

Founded in 2024, mag² spent two intensive years in laboratory development. We tested hundreds of topology-optimized lattices and laser-sintered iterations to achieve competition-grade 82g weight and seamless 45° corner-cutting.

Our Mission

Redefining Speedcubing Metallurgy

To break speedcubing free from disposable injection-molded plastics by pioneering aerospace additive manufacturing—crafting mechanical puzzles that deliver unmatched durability, tactile feedback, and acoustic perfection.
