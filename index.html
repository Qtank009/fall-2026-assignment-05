Metallurgical Innovation

Beyond Injection Molded Plastic

Traditional speedcubes rely on ABS plastic shells. At mag2, we harness Laser Powder Bed Fusion (LPBF) additive manufacturing to 3D print pure magnesium alloy. With a metal density of only 1.74 g/cm³, we achieved a featherlight competition weight of 82g while unlocking the acoustic resonance and structural rigidity only metal can provide.

82grams

Calculated Weight

Topology-optimized hollow internal ribbing and magnesium's ultra-low density keep total piece mass under 72g, reaching 82g fully assembled with hardware and magnets.

55.5mm

Standard Tournament Form

Built to standard World Cube Association competition dimensions. Zero compromises in ergonomics, hand feel, corner-cutting, or mechanical tolerance.

Neodymium Magnetic Core

Embedded N52 neodymium magnets inside internal piece chambers provide crisp, tactile snap alignment on every layer rotation with zero overshoot.
